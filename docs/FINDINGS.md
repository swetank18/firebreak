| 2026-09-09 | A | **Cause 1 — my power-law fitter was broken.** The x_min search scanned all unique cascade sizes and selected x_min=128, deep inside the finite-size cutoff (city has 1063 nodes, max cascade 213). Restricting x_min to a sensible range recovers **alpha = 1.51 at x_min=2, rising to 2.09 at x_min=20 — squarely inside the [1.3, 2.5] target.** The heavy tail was there all along; the estimator was reading the wrong part of the distribution. |

All 120 telecom towers had `telecom.bh_01` as their sole flow parent, making every tower a sibling of every other, so a single 120-node Motter-Lai redistribution group either barely moves or avalanches wholesale. That produces a *characteristic* cascade size, which is precisely the "threshold not a process" failure mode `docs/02-CITY-SPEC.md` warns is the subtle one. Real telecom has regional backhaul, not one national node. |

| 2026-09-09 | A/F | **Interventions shifted the RNG stream, which would have made the whole ablation measure noise.** The engine drew from one sequential generator, so protecting a single node changed every subsequent draw for every other node. | 2026-09-09 | A/F | **The realism gate checked cascade sizes and missed scenario saturation.** In a monsoon run, **986 of 1069 assets fail and 6.35M of 7.13M people are affected** — 89% of the city. | 2026-09-09 | A/F | **The oracle was misdefined and read BELOW arm A2** (prevented 1,788 against A2's 1,324,991), which is impossible for a ceiling. It ranked nodes by blame count — how many downstream failures they were credited with — but blame count is not damage. A node causing fifty small failures scores above one causing a hospital outage. | 2026-09-09 | A | **Tick size was changing the physics, and it had been changing it all along.** Exogenous ignition was `p = 0.0016 * stress * susceptibility` applied once per tick, so a discretisation choice set the ignition intensity: the same seed and scenario produced **611 exogenous ignitions at tick_s=60 against 177 at tick_s=600**. Every run in the project used tick_s=300 so nothing was inconsistent between results, but the number was arbitrary and nobody had chosen it. Replaced with a rate per second, `p = 1 - exp(-lambda * stress * susceptibility * tick_s)`. Ignitions now hold at 36.5 against 34.5 across the same 10x range in tick size. Residual sensitivity remains in cascade *speed* (a cascade advances one generation per tick either way), which is inherent to the discretisation; all evaluation is fixed at tick_s=300 and that is stated rather than left implicit. |
| 2026-09-09 | A | **The flood had no footprint.** `monsoon_flood` gave every asset in the city at least 35% of its exposure for the whole event, so a flood behaved like a city-wide degradation rather than an inundation of a region. Rebuilt as a water level that rises, peaks and recedes, where an asset is stressed only while the level exceeds its elevation. Assets on high ground are now never touched. |
