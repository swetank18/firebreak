| 2026-09-09 | A | **Cause 1 — my power-law fitter was broken.** The x_min search scanned all unique cascade sizes and selected x_min=128, deep inside the finite-size cutoff (city has 1063 nodes, max cascade 213). Restricting x_min to a sensible range recovers **alpha = 1.51 at x_min=2, rising to 2.09 at x_min=20 — squarely inside the [1.3, 2.5] target.** The heavy tail was there all along; the estimator was reading the wrong part of the distribution. |
| 2026-09-09 | A | **Cause 2 — a 120-member load-redistribution clique.** KS was genuinely rejecting (0.13-0.20 against a 0.011 critical value) because the CCDF has a bump around size 50: counts fall 104 at size 20, 38 at size 30, then rise to 135 at size 50. Traced it to flow topology, not dependency topology — dependency fan-out is healthy (max 6 dependents). All 120 telecom towers had `telecom.bh_01` as their sole flow parent, making every tower a sibling of every other, so a single 120-node Motter-Lai redistribution group either barely moves or avalanches wholesale. That produces a *characteristic* cascade size, which is precisely the "threshold not a process" failure mode `docs/02-CITY-SPEC.md` warns is the subtle one. Real telecom has regional backhaul, not one national node. |

| 2026-09-09 | A | **Cause 3, the real one — the system is SUBCRITICAL, and I nearly missed it by blaming my own gate.** After fixing the fitter and the telecom clique the gate still failed, so I tested whether the criterion itself was too strict (KS against an asymptotic critical value at n=15k rejects almost any imperfect fit — a known error that Clauset et al. exists to address). It is not too strict. A Vuong likelihood-ratio test of power-law against exponential favours **exponential at every x_min, decisively (p ~ 0)**. The tail is heavier than normal — max is 9.4 sd above the mean, P(size>=50)=0.083 — but it decays exponentially. In branching-process terms that is subcritical: rho(G) < 1, cascades die out geometrically. 52% of cascades are size 1 and the median is 1, which is consistent. A power law only emerges near criticality. Recorded before any parameter was touched. |
