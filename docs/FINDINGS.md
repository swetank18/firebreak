The tail is heavier than normal — max is 9.4 sd above the mean, P(size>=50)=0.083 — but it decays exponentially. In branching-process terms that is subcritical: rho(G) < 1, cascades die out geometrically. 52% of cascades are size 1 and the median is 1, which is consistent.
