It has to be a first-class field, not a layer-specific `attrs` entry, because the decision layer reasons about it and `decision/` may not read layer-specific attributes. |

| 2026-09-09 | A | **Two propagation mechanisms, deliberately.** Intra-layer is load redistribution (Motter-Lai): a failed node sheds load onto its siblings, and capacity = (1+tolerance) x initial load. This is the mechanism that produces heavy-tailed cascade sizes, which is what the realism gate checks for. Cross-layer is buffered dependency drawdown, which is what produces lead time and the diesel edge. One mechanism alone gives either a power law with no lead time, or lead time with no power law. |
| 2026-09-09 | A | **Layer physics (wntr/pandapower) deferred behind a resolver interface.** The cascade mechanism is the contribution; hydraulics are not. `city/engine/physics.py` defines the interface and ships a capacity-flow resolver. Real engines plug in later without touching the cascade loop. Stated in LIMITATIONS.md rather than implied. |
| 2026-09-09 | A | **Exogenous ignition is a rate per second, and hazard intensity is explicit and relative to the flood.** Only the monsoon has a real anchor — December 2015, roughly a third of the city inundated, about 60% losing power — so its intensity is 1.0 by definition and `IGNITION_RATE_HZ` is calibrated against it. heatwave (0.15), cyber (0.25), equipment_age (0.06) and none (0.05) have no comparable anchor and are calibrated to a stated operational profile instead. This is a judgement call and is labelled as one in LIMITATIONS.md. The alternative — leaving the magnitudes smeared through per-hazard exposure tables where nobody could see them — is how they came to be wrong in the first place. |
| 2026-09-09 | A | **Calibrating the simulator to contingency is not the same as tuning it to a result, and the distinction is worth stating because rule 8 exists.** No parameter here was moved to make an arm win. They were moved because every scenario was ending in total collapse, which makes the outcome non-contingent and every comparison meaningless — a validity precondition, not a finding. The power-law gate stayed failed throughout and is still reported as failed. |
| 2026-09-09 | A/C | **CONTRACT AMENDMENT: `Node.protected_class`.** Added so the decision layer can weigh a critical facility without naming a layer. Chennai sets it for the health layer; another city could mark shelters or schools. This is a genuine abstraction rather than a rename — what `decision/` needs to know is "this asset's outage is counted separately", not "this is a hospital". Amended after the hour-6 freeze, recorded here with the date, per the freeze rule. |
| 2026-09-09 | F | **The oracle is greedy over a candidate pool and evaluated by re-running the engine, and is reported as a lower bound rather than the optimum.** The pool deliberately contains every other arm's picks, so the ceiling can never be an artefact of the oracle not having been offered what an arm chose, and it is drawn from population, blame count and centrality so that roads — which serve no population and carry the diesel — are actually in it. An arm beating it would be a real result, not a bug. |
| 2026-09-09 | F | **The damage function lives in one module, `eval/damage.py`, imported by both the ablation and the console export.** Two copies would be two different headline numbers on the same slide, and the one on the map would quietly stop matching the one in the table. |
